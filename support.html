Support

Answers to common questions about Nobel Prize Winners. For anything else, write to arthuryess@gmail.com, or use Settings → Report a Bug in the app. Please include your device model, your iOS version and the app version (Settings → About → Version).

Data and offline use

Does the app work without an internet connection?

Yes, once it has downloaded the Nobel Prize data on first launch. After that you can browse, search, use the timeline and open your favorites offline. Photos and Wikipedia biographies you have already viewed are kept on the device. Ones you haven't opened yet need a connection.

How up to date is the information?

The laureate and prize data comes from NobelPrize.org. While the app is open, it checks for new laureates at most once an hour and refreshes everything at most once a day. New laureates announced in October appear as soon as NobelPrize.org adds them.

A photo or biography is missing or wrong.

Photos and biographies come from Wikipedia. Some laureates, especially organisations and early winners, have no photo there. If something looks wrong, email us the laureate's name and we will look into it.

Favorites

How do I add or remove a favorite?

Tap the star on a laureate or prize screen, or on a card in Search, Category or Timeline. Your favorites are in the Favorites tab. To remove several at once, tap Edit, select them and tap Delete.

Do my favorites sync between my iPhone and iPad?

No. Favorites are stored only on the device where you add them, and deleting the app deletes them.

Search

Tips for searching
  • You don't need accents: "Schrodinger" finds Schrödinger.
  • Combine words in any order: "curie physics" or "medicine 1901".
  • A year finds that year's prizes and laureates.

Notifications

What notifications can the app send?

Four optional types, all off until you turn them on in Settings → Notifications:

  • Favorites' Birthdays: birthdays of the laureates you starred.
  • Milestone Birthdays: 100th, 150th and 200th birthdays of any laureate.
  • Prize Centenaries: 100 years since a prize was awarded.
  • Nobel Day: the award ceremony on 10 December and Alfred Nobel's birthday on 21 October.

Notifications arrive at 10:00 local time on the day.

I turned a notification on but nothing arrives.

Check the iOS Settings app → Notifications → Nobel Prize Winners, and make sure notifications are allowed. A Focus mode can also hold them back. The app schedules the coming year of occasions each time you open it, so open it now and then.

Storage and appearance

How do I free up space?

Settings → Data & Storage → Clear Cache removes downloaded photos and Wikipedia biographies. They download again when you view them. Your favorites, recent searches and the Nobel Prize data stay.

How do I switch between light and dark mode?

Settings → Appearance → Theme: System, Light or Dark.
